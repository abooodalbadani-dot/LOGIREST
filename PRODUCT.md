## Users
LogiRest serves a diverse hierarchy of operational staff within a multi-branch restaurant ecosystem:
- **Kitchen Staff**: Real-time inventory consumers who need rapid, "glove-friendly" entry for issuing and receiving stock.
- **Inventory Managers & Warehouse Keepers**: High-stakes data curators who manage stock levels, transfers, and lot integrity.
- **Procurement Officers**: Decision-makers balancing supplier lead times with kitchen prep demand.
- **Approvers & Auditors**: Quality controllers who require a spotless, immutable "Precision Ledger" for compliance and financial oversight.

## Product Purpose
LogiRest is a high-integrity, ledger-based inventory management system designed for the rigorous demands of professional kitchens. Its core mission is to provide a "single source of truth" for every ingredient, lot, and movement, ensuring zero ambiguity in stock status and maximizing operational throughput while minimizing waste through aggressive FEFO (First-Expired-First-Out) logic.

## Brand Personality
- **Operational Elegance**: Sophisticated yet strictly functional. Like a Michelin-star prep station, every element is where it belongs, clean, and ready for high-speed action.
- **The Precision Ledger**: Mathematical, organized, and spotless. It conveys trust through absolute accuracy and an "immutable" history.
- **Authoritative Professionalism**: Calm under pressure. The interface does not scream for attention; it provides a steady, reliable foundation for rapid decision-making.

## Anti-references
- **Material Design Genericness**: Avoid the clinical, over-structured look of default "out-of-the-box" component libraries.
- **SaaS Clichés**: No "hero-metric" cards with neon gradients or 2014-era drop shadows.
- **Spreadsheet Fatigue**: It is a tool of precision, but it should never feel like a cold, generic Excel sheet.
- **1px Solid Borders**: Strictly prohibited for sectioning. Boundaries must be tonal.
- **Visual Noise**: Avoid heavy shadows, 100% black text, or decorative animations that slow down the data-entry workflow.

## Design Principles
- **Tonal Architecture**: Boundaries are defined through background color shifts between surface tiers, creating a "flush" hardware-integrated look that feels like part of the kitchen equipment.
- **Operational Integrity (The Ledger Rules)**: No physical deletion or editing of movements. Every stock change is a new record with a mandatory reason, lot number, and audit trail.
- **RTL-First Integrity**: Designed primarily for Arabic eye-flow, ensuring natural navigation and visual anchoring for the core regional audience.
- **Physicality of Depth**: Depth is achieved through the physical stacking of color tiers and ambient light metaphors, not artificial borders.
- **High-Density Efficiency**: Maximize visible data without sacrificing legibility. Every pixel must earn its place in the operational workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **Low-Light legibility**: High-contrast typography and specific "Dark Mode" (The Midnight Ledger) optimized for dim storage areas and back-of-the-house environments.
- **Glove-Friendly Targets**: Generous touch targets for high-frequency scan/entry points.
- **Motion Sensitivity**: Reduced motion by default for high-frequency data entry to minimize cognitive load and eye strain.
- **RTL Integrity**: Full bi-directional support with a strict RTL-first layout for the Arabic interface.
